# Economics Core Concepts
Tariffs are taxes on imports. Their incidence can be shared between buyers, sellers and other participants depending on market conditions.
Elasticity measures proportional responsiveness. Correlation describes co-movement and does not establish causation.
OLS estimates conditional relationships from the supplied data. ARIMA is a time-series forecasting family. Difference-in-Differences compares changes across treated and control groups and requires credible identification assumptions such as parallel trends.
