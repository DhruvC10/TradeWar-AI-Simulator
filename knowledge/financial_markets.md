# Financial Markets
A market index summarizes the performance of a defined basket of securities. Price levels should not be compared directly across different indices; percentage returns or indexed performance are more informative.
Volatility describes the dispersion of returns. Historical volatility is backward-looking.
Trade-policy shocks can affect firms through input costs, market access, demand, supply-chain disruption and expectations. A scenario shock in this app is an educational assumption, not a market forecast.
